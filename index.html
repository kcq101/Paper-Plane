Backyard Field Guide

Spot these before you throw.

Just for fun

  • Paper stars · tucked along the best lines: between rings, at the top of thermals, through the gate, deep in the canyon. Coral ones are new to you; pale ones you've already found. Find them all in each world.
  • The canyon · cross the long field, clear the hedge, and the yard drops into a giant canyon. Air rises everywhere, so you can float for a long time.
  • Rings · fly through for points. Hit the next one within a few seconds to build a streak, up to x5.
  • Birds · hitting one knocks you down and costs points. Skim past one for a close-call bonus.
  • Loop · snap the stick back fast while you have speed (a fresh throw or a dive). A slow pull just climbs. Each loop costs a little speed. Barrel roll · double-swipe left or right. It hops you sideways, and birds can't hit you mid-roll.

Harbor City

  • The city · fly down the main street or over the rooftops. Rooftop vents give warm lift, side streets can blow you sideways, and laundry lines will snag you.
  • The sea · a sea breeze rises off the seawall, and warm air keeps you up over the water. Thread the rings through the rock arches, and watch for seagulls and sailboat masts.

Lift

  • Grill · a tight column of hot air. Strong lift if you circle in it.
  • Sunspot · a yellow patch on the lawn. Gentle, wide lift that keeps going up high.
  • Trampoline · land on it and you bounce back up. Up to four boings.

Push and pull

  • Dust devil · a little tornado that wanders the yard. Spins you around and tosses you up. Shows up some throws, not all.
  • Box fan · on a picnic table. A hard shove in the direction it faces.
  • Crosswind · drifting scribbles across the middle of the yard. Pushes you to the right.
  • Sprinkler · heavy spray pushes you down. Only some are running each throw.

Ends your flight

  • Trees, shed, swing set · fly around them or over them.
  • Fence and hedge · the fence has an open gate dead ahead. The hedge marks the end of the yard.
  • Kiddie pool · splash! A soggy plane stops right there.

In the classroom

  • Heater vent · under the windows. Warm air lifts you, like the grills outside.
  • Sunbeam · bright patches on the floor. Gentle lift.
  • Ceiling fan · pushes you down and twists you. Not always running.
  • Open window · a breeze pushes you across the room.
  • Desk fan · on the teacher's desk, blowing back at you. A headwind.
  • Beanbag · bounces you back up. Trash can · land in it for a swish.
